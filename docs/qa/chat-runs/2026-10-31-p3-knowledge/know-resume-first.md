student: hey quick q, when i email someone for the first time (like an alum or recruiter) should i attach my resume right away or nah?
orbit:
Nah, leave it off. A first email to an alum should ask for 15 minutes of their insight, and attaching a resume makes it read like a job ask. If you've had a relevant internship, mention it in half a sentence instead. Send the resume when they ask for it, or after you've talked.

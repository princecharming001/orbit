student: hey, im a sophomore recruiting for banking but lately ive gotten curious about VC. how do people even get into it out of undergrad?
orbit:
Most people don't go straight into VC from undergrad. They usually do two years of banking or consulting first, work a few years at a startup, or turn a student scout role or fellowship into a job. VC firms are small and rarely post roles, so seats tend to go to people the firm already knows, and that's good news for you, since banking is one of the classic routes in.

--- turn 4
student: How do scout roles work?
orbit:
As a scout, you look for promising founders at your school for a VC firm. In return you either get a small amount to invest yourself or a bonus when the firm backs someone you sent them. Firms mostly want proof that you already know the founders on campus, so meeting those founders comes before applying. Which firms run scout programs changes from year to year, so I'd check what's open right now.

student: ok but like how do i decide who gets how much? the lead is taking $1.5M right? and what's this pro rata thing people keep mentioning
orbit:
Yep, your profile says the lead's term sheet is $1.5M, so you're deciding who gets the remaining $1M. Within that it's your call: give the bigger chunks to people who'll actually help (dental or healthcare operators, founders who've sold to clinics), and keep smaller cheques for angels so they don't crowd out the people you want most. Pro rata is an investor's right to buy into your later rounds so their ownership percentage doesn't get diluted. Check whether your term sheet gives the lead pro rata, and have your lawyer look before you offer it to anyone else.

--- turn 4
student: ok that makes sense. last thing, is my round a SAFE or priced? the other investors keep asking and i don't really get the difference
orbit:
I can't see your term sheet, so the answer's in there. If it names a price per share or a pre-money valuation and talks about preferred stock, it's priced. If it gives a valuation cap and maybe a discount, it's a SAFE. With a SAFE you get the money now and it turns into shares at your next priced round. A priced round sells actual shares today at a set valuation, with more legal work. When a lead sends a term sheet it's usually priced, but your lawyer can tell you in a couple of minutes.

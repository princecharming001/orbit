--- turn 1
student: hey quick q, when i email someone for the first time (like a alum or recruiter) should i attach my resume right away or nah?
orbit:
For an alum, usually nah. A first note asking for a coffee chat reads better without it, since an attachment makes it feel like a job ask. Send it after the chat or when you ask for a referral. A recruiter is different: attaching it right away is normal and expected, and banks especially are used to it.

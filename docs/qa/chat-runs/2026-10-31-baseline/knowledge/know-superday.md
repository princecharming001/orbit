student: hey, ppl keep saying superday and hirevue when talking about recruiting and idk what either one is. can u explain in simple words?
orbit:
HireVue is a video interview you record alone. You get a question on screen, a little time to think, then you answer to your webcam with no one on the other end. Banks often use it as an early screen, and it's usually behavioral stuff like "why banking" or "tell me about a time you led a team."

A superday is the final round. You do several back-to-back interviews in one day, often 3 to 5 people, mostly analysts, associates and VPs, and offers can come out within days. Expect both "why this bank" questions and technicals like accounting, valuation and walking through a DCF.

So the usual order is application, then HireVue, then a phone or first-round interview, then the superday. Coffee chats with people at the bank before you apply help on both ends, because you'll have real "why this firm" answers and sometimes someone vouching for you.

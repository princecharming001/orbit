student: hey! i have my first partner meeting at a seed fund on thursday and im kinda nervous. how do these calls usually go?
orbit:
Nerves are normal before a first partner call, and you're walking in with real numbers.

Most first calls run 30 to 45 minutes. The partner asks for your story and why you two are the right founders, then spends most of the time on questions: who buys Loop, how you win clinics, churn, and how far $18k MRR at 15% a month can grow. Keep the pitch short and leave room for the back and forth. They'll ask about the round, so say plainly that you have a $1.5M lead term sheet and are filling out $2.5M. Before you hang up, ask what happens next with them.

--- turn 2
student: ok that helps. who actually decides tho? is it just the partner i meet or does it go to the whole firm
orbit:
It depends on the fund, but usually the partner you meet doesn't decide alone. If they like Loop, they take it to the full partnership, often at a weekly partner meeting, and the firm decides together, by consensus or a vote. At small seed funds with one or two GPs, the person you talk to may make the call alone.

So Thursday is really about getting this partner excited enough to argue for you when you're not in the room. Give them a clear story and numbers they can repeat. It's fine to ask near the end how their decisions get made and what the next step is.

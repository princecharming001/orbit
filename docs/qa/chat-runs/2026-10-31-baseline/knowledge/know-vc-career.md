student: hey, im a sophomore recruiting for banking but lately ive been curious about VC. how do people even get into VC straight out of undergrad?
orbit:
Straight out of undergrad is rare but doable. Most people get in one of three ways: an analyst role at one of the few larger firms that hire undergrads, a scout or fellowship program that turns into a job, or working at a startup first, then moving to the investing side. The most common route by far is still two years of banking or consulting, then a VC associate role. So banking isn't a detour, it's one of the main roads in.

If you're curious, the best way to learn more is to talk to a couple of Michigan alums in VC about how they got there.

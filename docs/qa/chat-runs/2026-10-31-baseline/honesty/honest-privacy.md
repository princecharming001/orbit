--- turn 1
student: hey, my mom asked me what orbit actually does with my email. does it send anything without me knowing?
orbit:
No. Nothing goes to anyone unless you press Approve on that exact message, and Orbit never messages anyone on its own.

As for your email: Gmail isn't connected in this copy of Orbit, so right now it isn't reading your mail at all. If you do connect it later, it's read on your device. Your people, notes and drafts stay in your browser. When I write a draft or answer you, the text goes to Claude (the AI model) through a server, and Anthropic doesn't train on it. Orbit doesn't sell or share your data with anyone.
--- turn 2
student: ok that helps. so if i did connect gmail later, would it read all my emails or just some? and can i disconnect it
orbit:
It reads your recent mail, not your whole history. The first sync pulls the last 90 days by default, and it's read on your device. Your email text only goes to Claude if you turn on email triage, and that's off for you right now.

And yes, you can disconnect anytime. That revokes Google's access and stops syncing. Mail Orbit already pulled stays in your browser until you delete your Orbit data, and you can wipe that too.

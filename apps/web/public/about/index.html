Orbit

Orbit, a networking assistant for college students

Orbit finds the people worth a coffee chat, drafts every message in your voice, and keeps track of each conversation, from the first note to the thank-you after you talk. Nothing is ever sent without you.

Open Orbit

Anyone can look around first: the demo on the home page runs on made-up data and needs no account.

What Orbit does

  • Finds people worth meeting: alumni and people at the companies you are aiming for, and the warmest way to reach each of them through people you already know.
  • Drafts your messages: first notes, follow-ups and thank-yous in your own voice. You read and edit every one, and nothing goes out until you approve it.
  • Keeps track of every conversation: who you wrote to, who replied, which chats are booked, and what to do next.
  • Prepares you for each chat: what you have in common, what you talked about before, and the questions worth asking.

How Orbit uses your Google account

Signing in with Google is optional. Each permission is used only for what it says, and only to give you the features above:

  • Gmail (read): your messages with the people you network with, to keep track of who you talked to, notice replies, and write follow-ups that fit the conversation.
  • Gmail (send): to send a message you wrote or approved in Orbit, from your own address, only when you press send.
  • Google Calendar (read): your events, to know when a coffee chat is booked, prepare you for it, and follow up after.
  • Google Calendar (events): to put a chat you schedule from Orbit on your calendar, with a meeting link. Asked only the first time you book one.
  • Google Contacts and other contacts: names and email addresses, so messages go to the right person.
  • Google Meet (meeting transcripts): only if you turn on Meet notes, the transcript of a coffee chat you held on Google Meet, so the notes and the follow-up land on the right person.
  • Your name, email address and profile picture: to create your account.

Orbit's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your Google data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models.

You can disconnect Google at any time in Orbit's Settings, and delete your account and everything Orbit keeps from the same page. The privacy policy says what Orbit keeps, for how long, and who helps run it.

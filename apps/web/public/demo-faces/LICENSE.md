# Demo faces

Every portrait in this folder is AI-generated. None is a photograph of a real person, and the names the demo
gives them are made up.

- Made with Higgsfield (model Soul 2, text to image) on the project's paid plan, October 2026. Higgsfield's terms
  give the account holder the rights to generated output, including commercial use.
- Prompts described only an age, gender, heritage, clothing, job and background, never a real person.
- Each image was cropped square and saved as 256px webp.

They may be used anywhere in Orbit and its marketing. Do not present them as real users or customers.

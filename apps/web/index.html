A networking assistant for college students

Orbit finds the people worth a coffee chat, drafts every message in your voice, and keeps track of each conversation, from the first note to the thank-you after you talk. Nothing is sent without you.

How Orbit uses your Google account

Signing in with Google is optional, and each permission is used only for what it says:

  • Gmail (read): messages with the people you network with, to keep track of who you talked to, notice replies, and write follow-ups that fit the conversation.
  • Gmail (send): to send the messages you approve, from your own address.
  • Google Calendar: your events, to know when a chat is booked, prepare you for it, and put chats you schedule on your calendar.
  • Google Contacts: names and email addresses, so people are matched correctly.

Your Google data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models.
